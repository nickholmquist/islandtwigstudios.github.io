About

Hi, I’m Nick.

I’m a designer working out of Salem, Utah. IslandTwig Studios is the home for my brand identity work across sports teams, consumer brands, and naming projects. It’s also where the small iOS games I’ve built along the way live.

Most of my projects start with a word. A team name, a product hook, a feeling worth holding onto. From there the work becomes a mark, a system, a thing you can put on a jersey or hold in your hand.

Practice

  • Sports identity Hockey, baseball, and concept work for teams that don’t exist yet.
  • Brand & identity systems Logos, naming, type, color — the whole stack.
  • Product design iOS games and tools built in the same calm, tactile language.

Background

By day I work in technical success at a software company. It sounds unrelated, but the discipline of debugging complex systems sits closer to design than people think. Both come down to constraints, attention, and respect for how a thing wants to be used.

Let’s talk.

Open for identity work, brand systems, and naming. Always happy to hear from folks building something quietly ambitious.
