Most teams have a logo. Few have an identity that reflects who they are.

A logo marks you. An identity means something to the people who wear it.

Why this work matters

The best identities aren't designed in a vacuum. They're discovered.

When I played rugby, our branding never matched the pride we had for the team. The logo was generic, the merch was forgettable, and I always wished we had something people were actually excited to wear.

That stuck with me. The identities that last aren't pulled from a template. They come from the people, the place, and the traditions a team already carries.

I don't design logos for sports teams. I build identities that players wear with pride, fans rally behind, and communities remember.

The Legacy to New Era™ System

How an identity gets built.

01

02

03

Craft the System

Design the complete visual identity, from primary mark to the full set a team wears and lives in.

04

Launch into a New Era

Help the community embrace it through apparel, uniforms, and rollout.
