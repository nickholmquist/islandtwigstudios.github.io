The Process

01

Discover the Legacy

Every project begins with the Legacy Session, uncovering your organization's history, community roots, culture, and traditions.

02

Define the Identity

Research becomes strategy: core themes, symbolism, visual direction, and the design principles that guide every decision.

03

Craft the System

Strategy becomes identity: primary logo, wordmark, color palette, typography, and a complete set of brand guidelines.

04

Launch into a New Era

The identity is introduced to the world, with mockups, rollout guidance, and the story behind the mark.
