The problem

Most teams have a logo. Few have an identity that reflects who they are.

A team launches once. And when the mark doesn't mean anything, no one is excited to wear it, the community never rallies behind it, and it sets a ceiling that's slow and costly to recover from.

The work isn't the problem. The story is all there. It just hasn't been uncovered and built into something people can wear with pride.

  • You're entering a new chapter and the identity needs to carry real weight.
  • The old mark was pulled from a template and never felt like yours.
  • You know the history matters, but no one has turned it into an identity.

Why this work matters

The best identities aren't designed in a vacuum. They're discovered.

When I played rugby, our branding never matched the pride we had for the team. The logo was generic, the merch was forgettable, and I always wished we had something people were actually excited to wear.

That stuck with me. The identities that last aren't pulled from a template. They come from the people, the place, and the traditions a team already carries. That belief is the reason the Legacy to New Era System exists, and it's the reason every project starts with listening, not sketching.

What makes you different from other sports designers?

Most start with a sketch. I start with your story. Every visual decision comes out of the people, place, and traditions your team already carries, so the identity means something instead of just looking good.

Do you only design logos?

No. This is a full identity system, from the primary mark to the complete set a team wears and lives in. The logo is one part of a much larger identity.

What is the Legacy Session?

It's the free first conversation, and the front door to the whole system. We dig into your team's history and traditions so we both understand what the identity should stand for before any design begins. No cost, no commitment.

Do you work with teams outside Utah?

Yes. The work is remote and the process travels. If your team has a story worth building on, location isn't a barrier.
